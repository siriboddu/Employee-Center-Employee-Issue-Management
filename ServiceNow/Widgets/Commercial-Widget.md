# Commercial Widget

## Overview

The Commercial Widget is a custom ServiceNow Service Portal widget created for the Employee Center portal.

It provides a simple information section describing the services and benefits available through the portal.

## Widget Content

The widget displays:

- Our Commercial Services
- 24/7 Customer Support
- Faster Issue Resolution
- Transparent Tracking
- Secure & Reliable

## Purpose

The widget improves the Employee Center portal by providing users with a clear overview of the service experience.

## Portal Placement

The Commercial Widget was added to the Employee Center homepage in the left column of the two-column layout.

## Technology

The widget uses:

- HTML
- CSS
- ServiceNow Service Portal Widget framework
